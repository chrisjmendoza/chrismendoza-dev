RidePrint

A permanent service history and verification platform for bikes — the Carfax of bikes. Riders log every service record, sync rides from Strava, upload receipts that Claude Vision reads and pre-fills, and share a complete verified history by QR code. Shops log service directly to customer bikes behind a clock-in gate. Sellers list bikes with that verified history attached, which is a claim other marketplaces can't make. Privacy tiers are enforced in application code and again by Postgres row-level security.

Visit rideprint.com

Postmark

A privacy-first Android SMS client that fully replaces the default messaging app, keeping its own local database in sync with Android's system message provider. Photo, video and voice-memo messaging; full-text search; conversation stats and calendar heatmaps; scheduled backup and merge-only restore. No cloud, no accounts, no analytics. The MMS layer splits a carrier's byte budget across every attachment in a message and compresses to fit, rather than failing the send.

ShaftSchematic

An Android app for modeling marine propeller-shaft assemblies. Define a multi-segment shaft with bodies, tapers, threads, liners and bolt slots, watch a live dimensioned preview, and export one-page technical PDFs — schematics, runout sheets, wear documents, undercut drawings — without opening CAD. Built for machinists, shipyards and repair techs — and built out of my own day job inspecting and repairing marine shafts at a propeller shop.

Engine Visualizer

An interactive 3D visualizer for piston-engine geometry and motion. Enter bore, stroke, rod length and compression ratio, or load one of sixteen real engines, and watch the crank mechanism turn — using exact slider-crank kinematics rather than the usual sinusoidal approximation, so rod angularity is actually visible. Renders inline-3, -4 and -6 layouts at their real crank-throw phasing, plots position, velocity and acceleration from closed-form derivatives, and compares two engines side by side at a shared scale.

DocArchivist

A local-first, AI-powered document management system. Drop in a scanned PDF or a photo — DocArchivist extracts the text via OCR, applies AI-driven metadata (title, vendor, date, category), and files it into a searchable archive that never leaves the machine. Covers 22 document types and image-to-PDF ingest, ships as a Windows installer, and is backed by 865 automated tests and a nine-phase chaos plan for ingest resilience.

Writing & open source

Two full-length books that teach from real software rather than invented examples — both written against my own public Android codebases, with every quoted listing machine-checked against a pinned git tag.

Reading the Machine

377 pages · 16 chapters · appendices A–D

Teaches software architecture backwards: rather than building a toy system forwards, it walks through two real, public Kotlin/Android codebases and names what is already there. The reader is framed as a developer hired to build them — for a machine shop, and for the Android platform itself — so each architectural decision arrives with the client constraint that caused it.

Body complete · unpublished

Meeting the Machine

A first programming book · Kotlin from zero

The prequel. Most first books teach a construct and hang it on an invented toy, leaving an enormous gap between the last chapter and a real repository. This one teaches every construct twice — once in five lines you type and run, then again verbatim inside a forty-thousand-line working application, with its real file path. The goal is not to understand the whole listing; it is to recognize the thing you just learned, sitting in grown-up code.

Drafted · PDF and accessible HTML editions both build

About
